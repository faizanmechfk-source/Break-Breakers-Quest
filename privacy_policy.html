Privacy Policy for BREAK Breakers Quest

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 | Website: ysftech.in

Welcome to BREAK Breakers Quest, an arcade game developed by YSF TECH ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of our mobile application.

Summary: BREAK Breakers Quest is designed to be safe and privacy-friendly. We do not require account registration or collect direct personally identifiable information (such as your real name or email address). Game progress is stored locally on your device.

1. Information Collection & Storage

Our application collects minimal data required to function properly and display advertisements:

  • Local Game Data: High scores, unlocked stages, star ratings, and game preferences (sound/vibration toggles) are stored locally on your device via standard local storage.
  • Device & Usage Data: Technical information about your device, including device model, OS version, screen size, language preferences, and anonymized performance diagnostics.

2. Third-Party Advertisements (Ads)

To keep the game free to play, BREAK Breakers Quest may display third-party advertisements served by ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These ad networks may automatically collect and process certain data to display relevant ads:

  • Unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID).
  • IP address and general location data (country/city level).
  • Interaction data with displayed advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google handles ad data by reading the Google Privacy & Terms for Ads.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The app uses trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

4. Children's Privacy

BREAK Breakers Quest does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. In accordance with COPPA and Google Play policies, our advertising implementation adheres to age-appropriate content restrictions.

5. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You can control personalized advertising on your Android device at any time:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads on your Android device.
  • Select Reset Advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Security

We value your trust in using our game. Local save data remains on your physical device. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
